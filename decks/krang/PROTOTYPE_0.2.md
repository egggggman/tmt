# Krang Prototype 0.2

Status: **Smoke candidate**

Prototype 0.1 remains preserved in `PROTOTYPE_0.1.md` and `PROTOTYPE_0.1.txt`.

## Design Intent

Krang still accumulates machines as infrastructure for a calculated affinity-powered arrival. The
deck should remain tyrannical, resilient, and visibly distinct from Donatello, but resolving the
namesake refill/finisher should be a culmination rather than a routine repeated event.

## Evidence and decision

Calibration 0.1 put Krang at 63.7% (95% CI 60.5–66.8%) with eight matchups at 62–70%, 2.42 affinity
mana saved per game, and an affinity discount in 49.9% of games. Full execution was only 82.1%, so
the issue is broader resilience plus successful affinity turns rather than automatic setup alone.

One **Krang, Master Mind** becomes one **Ray Fillet, Man Ray**, and two **Does Machines** become two
**Bespoke Bō**. Reducing the namesake from three
copies to two lowers access to the affinity-powered hand refill and finisher while preserving it as
the deck's climax. Reducing Does Machines trims the recovery that made the infrastructure broadly
resilient. The third Ray Fillet and the two Bō maintain artifact infrastructure, so the change does
not dismantle the affinity plan. No permission, mana, or cheap artifact counts change.
