# Krang Prototype 0.1

Status: **Playable beta prototype**

## Thesis

Krang surrounds himself with machines, controls the pace, and arrives only when the infrastructure
can support him. Donatello builds artifacts to solve problems; Krang accumulates them as expendable
instruments of control and personal power.

## Play pattern and baseline questions

Cheap artifact creatures and surveillance devices build affinity while tapping, blocking, and
replacing themselves. Negate and Ooze Spill protect the infrastructure or deny opposing plans.
Does Machines recycles spent machinery. Krang then arrives cheaply, refills a depleted hand, and
turns every surviving artifact into additional power.

Test whether Krang arrives consistently but not trivially, whether the deck can actually close,
whether its permission is fun to face, whether four Does Machines clog, and whether the experience
feels tyrannical and calculated rather than like a second Donatello deck.
