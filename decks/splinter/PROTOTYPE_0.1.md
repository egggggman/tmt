# Splinter Prototype 0.1

Status: **Playable beta prototype**

## Thesis

Splinter teaches through discipline, sacrifice, and recovery. Small creatures learn to attack into
danger, leave the battlefield for value, and return wiser; Ninjas become the decisive students. The
deck should feel patient and resilient without becoming oppressive sacrifice control.

## Expected play pattern

Early students establish the board and make attacks or trades. Splinter strengthens the Ninja
contingent and uses Sneak to turn an unblocked student into a surprise mentor appearance. Pain 101
teaches survival through a dangerous exchange; Stomped by the Foot converts a disposable body into
a stronger answer; Tunnel Rats return after attrition.

Ninja Teen makes leaving the battlefield matter, then gives the class menace and eventually lets
fallen creatures return through Sneak. Foot Mystic rewards a carefully prepared Disappear turn.
Shark Shredder is the risky advanced student and late combat payoff. Splinter's Technique finds the
specific lesson the situation requires.

## Baseline hypotheses

1. Does sacrifice and return feel like mentorship, or merely generic black attrition?
2. Are there enough Ninjas for Hamato Yoshi's leadership bonus to matter?
3. Does Ninja Teen create satisfying progression without excessive tracking?
4. Is Pain 101 a memorable lesson or an awkward combat trick?
5. Can the deck pressure Donatello before value engines take over?
6. Does repeated recursion frustrate opponents?
7. Are four copies of Splinter too congested despite Sneak?
8. Does the deck create a concrete moment of Splinter teaching a student?
