# Shredder Prototype 0.1

Status: **Playable beta prototype**

## Thesis

Shredder treats every follower and resource as fuel for personal dominance. The deck deploys evasive
Foot soldiers, sacrifices expendable permanents, removes resistance, and lets its central threats
grow from everything that leaves the battlefield. Splinter teaches students to survive; Shredder
uses minions to make himself inevitable.

## Play pattern and baseline questions

Small evasive creatures and Oroku Saki establish pressure. Shredder's Armor turns disposable
permanents into weaponry; Stomped by the Foot and Shredder's Technique remove resistance while
feeding leave-the-battlefield triggers. Super Shredder grows from both players' departures. Foot
Mystic rewards planning a Disappear turn, and the larger Shredders convert unblocked attacks into
stolen resources or lethal deathtouch pressure.

Test whether sacrifice feels ruthless rather than fiddly, whether Super Shredder snowballs too
quickly, whether removal leaves meaningful counterplay, whether repeated Shredder legends clog the
hand, and whether the deck feels unmistakably villainous and distinct from Splinter.
