# Donatello Prototype 0.1

Status: **Playable prototype**

## Thesis

Donatello builds a network of small artifacts, learns from pieces that enter or break, and turns preparation into an adaptable battlefield advantage. The deck should feel inventive and technical without becoming passive draw-go control.

## Expected play pattern

Deploy a Fugitive Droid, Sewer-veillance Cam, Buzz Bots, or Crustacean Commando early. These pieces make artifacts matter while still developing the battlefield. Donatello, Way with Machines turns continued construction into a growing evasive threat; Gadget Master can Sneak through and copy the best device; Turtle Techie rewards having prepared an artifact; Mutant Mechanic converts a utility artifact into a Robot and preserves its work when it breaks.

Ooze Spill and Return to the Sewers are precise answers that also create Mutagen. Does Machines selects and recovers components. Donatello's Technique rewards finding an unblocked route and converts it into cards rather than raw damage.

## Prototype risks

1. Twenty-one creatures may be too few to pressure Leonardo or survive Raphael's removal.
2. The three-mana slot may clog despite the low early curve.
3. Gadget Master may lack a satisfying artifact to copy often enough.
4. Does Machines may be too slow against Raphael.
5. The deck may generate value without a decisive closing pattern.
6. Most importantly: does the pilot feel like they assembled a clever solution, or merely drew extra cards?
