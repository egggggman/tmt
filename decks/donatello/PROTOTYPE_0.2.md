# Donatello Prototype 0.2

Status: **Smoke candidate**

Prototype 0.1 remains preserved in `PROTOTYPE_0.1.md` and `PROTOTYPE_0.1.txt`.

## Design Intent

Donatello still builds a network of small artifacts and turns preparation into adaptable battlefield
advantages. The deck should remain inventive, technical, and proactive. Prototype 0.2 tests whether
the pilot can still assemble clever solutions when component recovery is less automatic.

## Evidence and decision

Calibration 0.1 put Donatello at 63.2% (95% CI 60.1–66.4%). Setup occurred in 98.8% of games,
payoffs were realized in 93.8%, and full execution occurred in 92.0%. The deck averaged 2.77
realized payoffs per game. This supports a reliability intervention, not a rebuild.

Two copies of **Does Machines** become two **Negate**, and two **Donatello's Technique** become two
additional **Return to the Sewers**. Does Machines both selects and recovers
components and is modeled as an artifact payoff; reducing it from three copies to one attacks the
repeated setup-to-payoff conversion identified by calibration. Technique turns an unblocked route
into additional cards, so replacing it removes another repeated conversion layer while Return keeps
Donatello's precise, Mutagen-producing answer package. Negate preserves blue technical
problem-solving and gives the pilot a deliberate protection choice. Neither replacement builds
artifacts or recycles the engine. All creature versions of Donatello, all fourteen artifact setup
pieces, and the mana base remain intact; only two spells move from mana value two to three.

## Smoke questions

1. Does aggregate strength move toward the 45–55% target without erasing the invention engine?
2. Do setup and payoff realization remain coherent but less automatic than 98.8% and 93.8%?
3. Do the four prior polarity matchups above 65% contract?
4. Does Negate create meaningful protection decisions rather than passive draw-go play?
