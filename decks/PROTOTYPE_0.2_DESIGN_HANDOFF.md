# Prototype 0.2 Design Studio Handoff

Status: **Donatello and Krang revised; all other Prototype 0.1 decks frozen**

## Calibration evidence accepted

Design Studio accepts Cardcade Calibration 0.1 as evidence for two surgical revisions: Donatello's
over-reliable artifact conversion and Krang's broad strength with affinity-supported resilience.
Cardcade reported the evidence; Design Studio owns the changes documented in each Prototype 0.2.

The twelve Calibration 0.1 matchups outside 35–65% are retained as smoke watchpoints. Nine involve
Donatello or Krang. The other three—Raphael over Leonardo, Raphael over Bebop & Rocksteady, and
Shredder over Leonardo—remain watchlist items and do not authorize unrelated deck changes.

## Frozen decisions

- Donatello: two `Does Machines` become two `Negate`; two `Donatello's Technique` become two
  additional `Return to the Sewers`.
- Krang: one `Krang, Master Mind` becomes one `Ray Fillet, Man Ray`; two `Does Machines` become two
  `Bespoke Bō`.
- Leonardo and Splinter: unchanged pending results after reducing dominant-deck pressure.
- Bebop & Rocksteady: unchanged. Its 24-land construction, low screw rate (0.3%), high flood rate
  (37.0%), cycling top end, and coarse turn-eight flood definition make simulator interpretation a
  live explanation. Human logs should record lands drawn, cycling choices, and unused mana before
  Design Studio changes the list.
- Shredder: unchanged; the 50.3% calibration result clears the aggregate smoke concern.
- Every other Prototype 0.1 list: unchanged.

## Promotion gate

The Prototype 0.2 roster must pass structural validation and the exact 20-game-per-matchup,
balanced-start smoke protocol. A new 100-game calibration is not authorized until smoke is stable,
credible, and explained against the Calibration 0.1 hypotheses.
