# Physical Deliverables Register

Mr. Paperback owns physical-product specifications and validation. A deliverable is not complete
because a digital file exists; it must pass the relevant print, cut, fold, fit, durability, and play
tests.

## Starter-set deliverables

- ten individual deck boxes;
- one master collector box;
- Avery OL1067WR label sheets where that stock remains appropriate;
- deck dividers;
- token cards;
- damage and counter tokens;
- a life counter;
- Field Manual;
- quick-reference cards;
- matchup tracker;
- first-player token;
- character and art cards;
- promotional and membership materials.

## Validation states

Each item progresses independently through **specified**, **print prototype**, **cut/fold
prototype**, **fit verified**, **durability verified**, **play verified**, and **production ready**.
Only applicable states are required, but skipped states must be explained.

Dimensions, quantities, stock, finishes, artwork, and manufacturing methods belong in the owning
item specification. This register records scope, not invented production details.
