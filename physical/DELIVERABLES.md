# Physical Deliverables Register

Mr. Paperback owns physical-product specifications and validation. A deliverable is not complete
because a digital file exists; it must pass the relevant print, cut, fold, fit, durability, and play
tests.

## Master collector / transport case prototype checkpoint

Mr. Paperback has selected a **Deck Fortress-style hard-shell case** as the current master-case
prototype and possible long-term solution. The decision is intentionally practical: keeping the ten
playable decks protected, organized, and easy to transport has higher priority than fabricating a
custom outer shell during the current prototype stage.

The current candidate is the **TheThirdFilament Deck Fortress** described by its maker as a hard-shell
ABS case with a 3D-printed interior insert, carrying handle, locking clasps, a toploader display slot,
and capacity for 10 or 12 standard-sized TCG decks. The maker lists exterior dimensions of
**16.5 in × 4.5 in × 13 in** and describes each deck position as suitable for roughly 100+ double-sleeved
cards. These are vendor-provided specifications and remain unverified by Mutants the Gathering until
the physical unit is received and measured.

The 3D-printed interior is a major reason for selecting this prototype direction. The outer hard case
may remain stable while interior modules can be replaced, duplicated, or redesigned as the physical
product develops. Future inserts may provide dedicated organization for the ten decks, tokens,
counters, life counter, first-player marker, dice, spare sleeves, Field Guide / rules-reference
materials, and other Mr. Paperback accessories.

The current stock configuration appears to provide ten deck positions plus accessory storage, which
aligns well with the ten-deck battle set. **Do not assume the existing wells fit the official TMNT metal
tins.** The initial acceptance target is safe organization of the sleeved playable decks. The official
TMNT tins remain a separate collectible / individual-deck-container track unless physical measurement
proves that a combined workflow is practical.

This is a **prototype selection, not a production-final manufacturing decision**. Do not design custom
replacement inserts from exterior dimensions or photographs alone. Once the case is in hand, record
the actual interior dimensions, deck-well dimensions, accessory-bin dimensions, lid clearance, card
fit, and transport behavior before authorizing any custom 3D-printed Mutants insert.

### Master-case validation path

**candidate selected → physical case received → OL600SP deck-holder label fit test → dimensions recorded →
10-deck fit verified → accessory layout tested → transport / closure test → custom insert need assessed →
durability verified → production decision**

### Deck-holder label fit test

Before creating a new label size, test one existing **OL600SP 4 in × 2.5 in** Mutants deck label
against a Deck Fortress deck holder. Record whether it fits the usable face without overhang, whether
the rounded corners or edges interfere with card access or the holder opening, and whether the deck
artwork remains readable when installed in the case.

This is a **fit/no-fit experiment only**. Do not trim the established OL600SP label standard or
redesign the existing label artwork to compensate for the Deck Fortress. If the label does not fit,
measure the holder's actual usable flat area (width × height) and create a separate Deck Fortress
label format while preserving the OL600SP tin-label standard unchanged.

Preserve the stock insert and any future replacement-insert revisions as prototype history rather
than overwriting earlier configurations.

## Sewer Stamps prototype checkpoint

The first Sewer Stamps set is accepted into the Mr. Paperback physical-product suite as a **print prototype**, not a production-final design. It establishes the direction for future sticker work: late-1980s/early-1990s cartoon styling, vibrant colors, deck-specific character stamps, mana-identity cues, and additional turtle/sewer-themed promotional designs.

The supplied Premium Label Supply PLS619 sheet is the mechanical truth for this product. Artwork must be designed for and placed into its physical 2 in × 1.5 in label media; the template geometry must not be changed to accommodate artwork.

The current prototype artifact is `PLS619_90s_sewer_stamps_placed_v0_8.pdf`. Preserve it as historical prototype evidence. The next gate is a physical print/alignment test before any claim of production readiness.

## Validation states

Each item progresses independently through **specified**, **print prototype**, **cut/fold
prototype**, **fit verified**, **durability verified**, **play verified**, and **production ready**.
Only applicable states are required, but skipped states must be explained.

Dimensions, quantities, stock, finishes, artwork, and manufacturing methods belong in the owning
item specification. This register records scope, not invented production details.
