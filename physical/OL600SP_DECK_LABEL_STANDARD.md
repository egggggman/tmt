## Printing rule

Print at **100% / Actual Size**. Do not use Fit to Page, Shrink to Printable Area, or other scaling.

The label geometry is mechanical truth. Artwork must be composed for the label rather than changing, cropping, or stretching the template to accommodate artwork.

## Accepted graphic setup

The physically validated sheet uses a consistent eight-label, character-deck presentation:

1. large **Mutants the Gathering** identity;
2. character-forward environmental illustration;
3. large deck / character nameplate;
4. short deck-identity descriptor;
5. TMNT recognition element;
6. compact mana / deck-identity symbol area;
7. thick black cartoon outlines;
8. saturated flat / cel-style color;
9. simplified late-1980s / 1990s cartoon-comic rendering;
10. subtle environmental easter eggs.

The accepted graphic structure is intentionally **simple, functional, collectible**. Background scenes may vary by deck, but the information hierarchy and overall framing should remain consistent across the set.

Deck-specific rules text, mana identity, or strategic claims remain owned by Design Studio and must be checked against the current deck source of truth before final production. This physical validation locks the **print geometry and graphic system**, not an independently invented gameplay specification.

## Change control

This standard is frozen. Do not casually alter the dimensions, pitch, margins, rounded-corner geometry, or overall label hierarchy.

A change requires one of:

- a documented physical printing failure;
- a verified media / printer incompatibility;
- an explicit Mr. Paperback revision decision followed by another physical print test.

Earlier failed, cropped, stretched, or misregistered attempts remain development history and must not replace this validated baseline.
