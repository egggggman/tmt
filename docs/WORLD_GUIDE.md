# World Guide

## Purpose

The world layer presents TMNT Design Studio as a living underground community. It creates continuity,
belonging, humor, and reasons to return while remaining separate from analytical software.

## The living underground community

The community is larger than its heroes. Residents work, trade, argue, celebrate, solve problems,
remember losses, and build traditions. The world evolves naturally through recurring people, places,
businesses, events, and consequences rather than one rigid master plot.

## Relationship to the software

The world supports the software by making research and design approachable. It may explain a release,
celebrate a milestone, or invite a reader to investigate evidence. It never changes Magic Facts,
Capabilities, Deck Metrics, Findings, Alignment, or recommendations. Analytical software remains
invisible in-universe.

## Community institutions

- **The Underground Press** informs, connects, celebrates, and preserves community history under
  the rules in [its publication guide](UNDERGROUND_PRESS.md).
- **Puzzle Dojo** offers crosswords, Sudoku, ciphers, mazes, logic puzzles, word searches, and other
  playful challenges that reward curiosity.
- Familiar homes, workshops, shops, kitchens, transit routes, gathering spaces, and services may
  recur when stories earn them.

## Traditions and recurring life

Community meals, repair days, memorials, markets, performances, training exhibitions, seasonal
events, and mutual-aid efforts may return over time. Recurrence should build familiarity rather than
fill a quota. Small details can mature across many appearances.

## Reader participation

Letters, puzzle responses, questions, corrections, classifieds, notices, and community submissions
let readers participate without making them spectators to a hero-only story. Participation must be
safe, welcoming, credited where appropriate, and consistent with privacy rules.

## Canon rules

1. Never break the fourth wall in an in-universe piece.
2. Keep software, databases, engines, repositories, and release mechanics invisible in-universe.
3. Do not present fan interpretation as official TMNT or Magic canon.
4. Respect established project continuity; correct contradictions transparently.
5. Never reveal details that could endanger the underground community.
6. Community history persists. Later stories remember material earlier events.
7. Heroes matter, but the community is not scenery for them.

## Living-world principle

Not every thread needs a planned conclusion. A shop may expand, a rivalry may soften, a repair may
fail again, or a festival may acquire new customs. Reward attentive readers with callbacks and
evolving detail while leaving room for surprise.

## Rule of Joy

The world should make people curious, connected, and glad they visited. When continuity becomes a
burden rather than a source of discovery, simplify the machinery and return attention to community
life.
