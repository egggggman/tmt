The interpreter admits only a complete level-three section. The engine creates a general spell-cast event after costs and announcement for ordinary, Sneak, or linked casts. A Class at level three with its ability active places a trigger above that spell. The trigger retains the original controller and cast-event provenance through source departure or control change; on resolution it puts a +1/+1 counter on **each creature currently controlled** by that player. Empty teams remain legal. Ability suppression prevents new triggers. Earlier level-one attack and level-two departure behavior remains active. Incomplete or misplaced sections leave level three unavailable.
