## Classification rules

- **must resolve before calibration:** a material omitted gameplay family should block unrestricted printed-rules ten-deck calibration. This is a conservative recommendation from source and exposure, not proof it changed a winner. Resolve it with accepted semantics before that experiment, or obtain HQ approval for a genuinely different prospective question whose claims exclude it. It does not block preparation.
- **can defer:** defer implementation now because this artifact does not establish the specific claimed opportunity/material consequence strongly enough. Retain it as an unresolved applicability/fitness risk in protocol preparation. This is not a permanent waiver: if the intended question depends on it, an explicit closure decision is needed before calibration. Low frequency alone is never the reason to defer.
- **terminal-artifact only:** the saved game ended before an already represented obligation could finish. No missing implementation is inferred from that terminal row; never force post-game execution or award false credit.

Exposure below is raw distinct games / occurrences, not independent statistical samples or proven beneficial activations. Rows overlap games. Classification partitions the 111 rows into **67 must-resolve occurrences across 51 games**, **36 deferrable occurrences across 24 games**, and **8 terminal-only occurrences across 8 games**. These game totals are overlapping and must not be summed or used to relabel the official 66 coverage-limited games.

## Concrete applicability corrections

**Tunnel Rats:** `april_oneil--splinter:canonical:8017`, source `object-000156`, occurrence `semantic-000005`, `context-000001`: source and subjects are battlefield objects, not the graveyard card. The accepted Tunnel Rats audit remains controlling. Do not re-promote it through a raw ranking.

**Frog Butler:** `april_oneil--bebop_rocksteady:canonical:8001`, source `object-000143`: `semantic-000002` (mana) and `semantic-000003` (Reach) share `context-000001`. Its facts specify two mana and no tap-source cost. Every one of the 15 mana witnesses has these same cost/tap facts. `engine07.py::_witness_unsupported_activation_contexts` creates a fixed-cost context; `_witness_from_context` accepts colon-shaped fragments without binding the exact activation cost/fragment. A legal context for one ability cannot certify another ability on the same card. No telemetry correction is implemented by this audit.

**Michelangelo:** `april_oneil--michelangelo:canonical:8012`, source `object-000156`, `semantic-000005`, `context-000001`, turn 13 precombat main: `counter_type: stun`, `quantity: 1`. `place_counters` and the broad replacement applicability predicate admit this context even though the printed replacement concerns +1/+1 counters. The artifact authenticates what was recorded; it does not make that predicate correct.

For Leonardo, Paramecia and Raphael, the table distinguishes an authenticated permission/death/entry/attack from a demonstrated payable or beneficial use. Those are source-supported material legal-choice families, but the matrix does not estimate their causal calibration impact. No percentage of winner changes is claimed.
