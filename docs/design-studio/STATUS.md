## Evidence boundary

Cardcade reports reproducible observations and hypotheses. It does not redesign decks, authorize a
revision, or turn simulated win rates into deck-quality grades. Design Studio decides whether
credible simulator evidence and human play experience justify a specific change.

## Revision gate

A new prototype requires:

1. a named problem supported by inspectable evidence;
2. a statement of whether the problem belongs to the simulator, deck, theme, product, or play
   experience;
3. the smallest proposed change and its expected tradeoffs;
4. explicit Design Studio authorization;
5. a new preserved prototype version rather than mutation of an earlier deck.

Until those conditions are met, current Cardcade findings remain learning inputs only.
