## Design intent
Bad Guys is the unruly, expendable muscle of Shredder's organization. Bebop & Rocksteady headline an aggressive, combat-forward Foot Clan/mutant henchman shell. Preserve the separate Shredder deck's mastermind/interaction identity and Krang's artifact engine. This is NOT a catch-all villains deck.

These are research leads, not assertions of legality or final inclusions:

## Existing evidence
R9-B vs Baseline 005: 323/900 (35.89%) vs preserved control 316/900 (35.11%), +0.78 percentage points. Control raw 4,500-game artifact not independently verified. Weak matchups: Splinter 17/100, Casey 28/100, Raphael 31/100. No causal deck-design inference from win rate alone.

## Mana-curve and role analysis
- Preserved R9-B: 20 lands / 40 nonlands; official Turtle Team-Up mono-color hero decks: 24 / 36. Their co-op enemy deck is 38 cards and cannot be directly transplanted.
- Prior exploratory black-green 60-card sandbox was reported as 20 lands, 30 creatures, zero one-mana spells, and six 5–6-mana cards. Recover its exact immutable decklist before claiming card-level comparison.
- Zero one-drops risks passing turn one; 20 lands with six 5+ mana spells risks stranded threats. These are hypotheses requiring opening-hand and land-drop telemetry, not established failure mechanisms.
- Build a computed mana-value histogram from an Oracle-verified list (1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6+; lands separate). Measure color-pip demand and early B/G source access; compare 20, 22, and 24-land alternatives only as isolated hypotheses, not simultaneous uncontrolled edits.
- Roles to quantify: one/two-turn board presence, expendable bodies, attacks and combat pressure, removal, card flow, top-end closers, land count.

## Synergy assessment
1. Foot bodies -> early attackers, board density and disposable resources. Verify actual abilities and triggered actions.
2. Bebop & Rocksteady -> featured brute-force finishing pressure. Avoid dilution by unrelated villain legends.
3. Combat payoff -> aggressive attacks, menace/trample/forced-block or attack effects where Oracle-supported; inspect whether forced actions are implemented correctly.
4. Removal -> clear blockers while advancing board. Verify the pilot actually selects useful targets and actions.
5. Weak-matchup response -> Splinter, Casey, Raphael: compare game length, land drops, early creatures, board wipes/removal, and ending board states before assigning causes.
