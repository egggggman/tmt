# Canon and Source Material Charter

Canon and Source Material supplies attributed thematic research to Design Studio and Mr. Paperback.
It preserves the boundary among official source material, project interpretation, and the fictional
community framing governed by the [World Guide](../WORLD_GUIDE.md).

## Responsibilities

- research Characters, relationships, settings, objects, events, language, and visual motifs;
- record what a source directly supports and what remains interpretation or uncertainty;
- maintain a prioritized research queue tied to a real deck, product, or editorial need;
- hand off concise, attributed findings to the department making the decision;
- flag conflicts without presenting literal fidelity as automatic gameplay authority.

## Evidence and attribution

Every durable research note identifies the source title, edition or version when relevant, creator or
publisher, publication date when known, and a locator such as episode, issue, page, scene, or URL.
Notes label direct observation, quotation, synthesis, inference, disputed continuity, and unknowns.
Quotations remain brief and rights-respecting; summaries are preferred.

## Research queue

A queue item states the requesting department, decision it supports, scope, priority, known sources,
open questions, and completion condition. Curiosity without a current dependency may be recorded as
backlog, but it does not displace decision-critical research.

## Handoff format

Each handoff includes the question, concise findings, evidence table, continuity or rights caveats,
interpretive options, recommendation if requested, and unresolved questions. Design Studio retains
gameplay authority; Mr. Paperback retains production authority; Canon retains source-accuracy
authority.
