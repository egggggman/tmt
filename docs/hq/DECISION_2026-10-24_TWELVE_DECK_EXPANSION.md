# HQ Decision — Twelve-Deck Expansion Model

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Authority:** Explicit human project-owner instruction in HQ conversation.
**Decision status:** ACCEPTED DIRECTION; recorded on draft governance branch pending merge to main.
**Related:** Issue #359, PRs #362, #363, #364; frozen Baseline 005.

## Decision

The target collection is **twelve distinct Standard-legal 60-card TMNT decks**.

1. Preserve all **ten existing Baseline 005 decks**, including **Bebop & Rocksteady** as its own independent character deck.
2. Develop **Baxter Stockman — Baxter's Mutant Menagerie** as the **eleventh** distinct deck candidate.
3. Develop **Bad Guys — Shredder's Cronies** as the **twelfth** distinct deck candidate.
4. **Shredder's Cronies does not replace Bebop & Rocksteady.** The replacement interpretation in Issue #359 is superseded *as roster direction only*. Research from that issue/PR #363 remains valuable and preserved.
5. Neither candidate is promoted or designated Game Night Ready solely by this decision. Each requires card identity, exact 60-card list, Standard legality, structural integrity, semantic readiness, and appropriately authorized playtesting.
6. The ten existing deck versions, Baseline 005 manifest, R9-B experiment, runtime identities and historical evidence remain unchanged and authoritative for their historical context.

## Department implementation boundaries

- **HQ** owns the final roster, authorization, independent acceptance and Game Night First goals.
- **Design Studio** owns the two candidate lists and separate identities: Baxter artifact/Robot/Mouser synergy; Cronies villain/henchman ensemble distinct from Shredder, Krang and B&R. Design Studio may test hypotheses but must not overwrite or auto-promote Baseline 005.
- **Cardcade** assesses action coverage, engine validity, deterministic simulations and matchup evidence under independently reviewed runtime and explicitly authorized test gates; it cannot autonomously redesign decks.
- **Mr. Paperback** plans future 12-deck capacity while deferring final box counts, dimensions, print production and artwork until candidate readiness and prototypes are confirmed.
- **Canon** protects recognizable thematic identity without compromising gameplay.
- **Underground Press** may support later public Game Night materials without affecting gameplay decisions.

## Testing milestones

**Game Night Ready:** all twelve decks complete, Standard-legal, physically playable and teachable, with severe failure modes triaged. Early human play can occur before perfect automated balance.

**Refined Battle Set:** accurate simulator validation plus logged human enjoyment and fairness evidence support bounded deck refinements. No claim of a finished twelve-deck matchup matrix is made now.
