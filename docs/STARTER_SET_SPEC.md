# Ten-Deck Starter Set Specification

This document defines the product-level acceptance boundary for the Mutants the Gathering ten-deck
battle set. [HQ](HQ.md) coordinates the product; specialist departments retain authority over their
artifacts.

## Contents

- ten distinct 60-card, Standard-legal Sewer Decks with preserved version history;
- the physical items in the [Physical Deliverables Register](../physical/DELIVERABLES.md);
- rules, learning, setup, matchup-tracking, and reference material sufficient to use the set;
- attributed character, art, and source-material decisions appropriate to each artifact.

## Department acceptance

- **Design Studio:** deck identity, Design Intent, legality, prototype status, and revisions.
- **Cardcade:** reproducible simulator evidence and stated validation limits.
- **Canon / Source Material:** attributed thematic support and continuity caveats.
- **Mr. Paperback:** print specifications and physical validation.
- **Underground Press:** separate editorial coverage when a real accepted milestone warrants it.
- **HQ:** cross-department completeness and unresolved dependency tracking.

## Production-ready gate

The set is production-ready only when:

1. all ten deck versions and Design Intents are explicitly accepted;
2. objective analysis and credible play evidence are preserved without claiming matchup guarantees;
3. human playtesting addresses clarity, enjoyment, identity, and repeated-play concerns;
4. every required physical deliverable has passed its applicable validation states;
5. rules, references, rights, attribution, accessibility, and safety checks are complete;
6. unresolved limitations and intentionally deferred work are visible.

Cardcade output alone cannot satisfy this gate. A digital mockup alone cannot satisfy physical
validation. No component is silently declared complete because another department has advanced.
