# Mutants the Gathering — Field Manual Completion Guide v0.1

**Owner:** Mr. Paperback  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT  
**Current prototype:** Field Manual 0.1 — Foundation

## Purpose

This document is the durable completion guide for the Mutants the Gathering Field Manual. It defines the manual's structure, visual direction, physical-product expectations, content-stability model, and prototype path.

The Field Manual does not need to wait for every deck and Cardcade result to become final. Stable material should be built now. Material dependent on final deck construction, accepted simulation evidence, or later human playtesting remains modular and provisional until the responsible department settles it.

> **Build the chassis now. Fill in final gameplay details when the evidence is ready.**

## Department boundaries

Mr. Paperback owns the Field Manual as a physical deliverable: format, layout, print behavior, artwork integration, assembly, readability, and production files.

Design Studio owns final deck identity, deck construction, card-specific strategy, and deck revisions.

Cardcade may provide accepted simulation evidence and development history. It does not author deck revisions.

Canon supports thematic accuracy and character identity.

HQ coordinates cross-department dependencies and durable project decisions.

## Reusable deck-chapter template

Each deck chapter should use the same underlying information architecture while retaining its own visual personality:

1. Character
2. Deck Identity
3. How This Deck Fights
4. Signature Mechanics
5. Key Interactions
6. Tokens / Reminders
7. Tips / Things to Watch

The template exists to make ten distinct decks easier to learn, not to make their presentations identical.

## Visual direction

The Field Manual should feel like an artifact from Mutants the Gathering rather than a generic instruction booklet.

Core direction:

**1990s TMNT energy + sewer field manual + underground zine + technical handbook.**

Potential visual vocabulary includes:

- sewer infrastructure and utility diagrams;
- photocopy and halftone texture;
- taped or pasted-in material;
- handwritten annotations;
- stamps and graffiti;
- pizza stains and mutant markings;
- technical callouts;
- deliberately printed imperfections;
- restrained Magic-inspired visual references where appropriate;
- the established Mutants the Gathering visual identity.

Artwork and decoration must support readability. Reference and rules pages should remain fast to use at the table.

## Physical-product rule

The Field Manual is a physical product first.

Development should consider:

**trim size → page count → spreads → binding → margins → bleed → paper → print → fold/bind → handling → readability**

A screen PDF that looks attractive but prints, trims, binds, or handles poorly does not pass Mr. Paperback validation.

Physical prototypes are required before production approval.

## Content stability system

Every section or page should be classified during development.

### STABLE

Safe to develop toward finished quality now. Its authority is not expected to change as deck balancing continues.

### PROVISIONAL

The structure and design may be developed, but some content depends on another department or an unfinished project milestone.

### GATED

Final content must not be written or represented as settled until its authoritative evidence or decision exists.

Examples:

- A deck chapter's visual template may be STABLE while final card-specific strategy remains PROVISIONAL.
- Final Cardcade calibration statistics are GATED until the corresponding evidence is accepted.
- Final deck lists and strategy text follow Design Studio authority.

This system exists to prevent unnecessary manual redesign when another project layer changes.

## Prototype completion path

### 0.1 — Foundation

Establish physical specification, information architecture, visual bible, cover concepts, reusable templates, and representative interior spreads.

### 0.2 — Skeleton

Produce a complete paginated manual with every planned section represented, including clearly marked placeholders.

### 0.3 — Content Build

Populate stable rules, product, character, league, and reference content.

### 0.4 — Deck Integration

Insert Design Studio's accepted deck identities and sufficiently stable gameplay guidance.

### 0.5 — Cardcade Integration

Insert accepted Cardcade development material and any final statistics selected for the player-facing manual.

### 0.6 — Complete Draft

Remove structural placeholders and complete artwork and editorial passes.

### 0.7 — Physical Prototype

Print, assemble, handle, and use the manual during actual games.

### 0.8+ — Print Refinement

Correct readability, pagination, color, binding, artwork, paper, and other physical issues discovered through testing.

### 1.0 — Production Field Manual

Approve the physical and digital production master associated with the completed starter/battle set.

## Definition of done

The Field Manual is not complete merely because every page contains text. It must pass four gates.

### Content

Accurate and sufficient to serve players.

### Design

Cohesive, readable, and unmistakably Mutants the Gathering.

### Gameplay

Genuinely useful while learning and playing the ten-deck environment.

### Physical

Prints, trims, binds, opens, handles, and survives normal tabletop use correctly.
