# Krang Round 6 — Design Studio closure

**Owner:** 🧪 Design Studio  
**Status:** CLOSED_REJECTED_FAMILY_PRESERVED

## Decision

R6-A, R6-B, and R6-C are preserved rejected experiments. None is eligible for combined validation or promotion.

R6-C's Retro-Mutation mechanism executed correctly after semantic readiness work. Its 900-game isolated comparison improved aggregate Krang WR but worsened matchup distribution, including new or worsened polarization against April O'Neil and Leonardo. Therefore the deck result is rejected even though the engine work and evidence are valid.

## Closed hypothesis family

**Falsified for Round 6:** a one-card replacement of one dormant Negate, whether proactive material, artifact stabilization, or threat suppression, is sufficient to smooth Krang's matchup distribution.

This statement is intentionally narrow. It does not mean Negate can never be cut, that the tested cards are inherently bad for Krang, or that one-card changes are universally invalid. It closes only the tested Round 6 family.

## Transition to Round 7

Round 7 may consider the full frozen Standard-legal pool and may change more than one slot when a coherent package is required. Candidate generation should follow:

diagnose mechanism → search full legal pool → screen theme/role/semantics → choose smallest evidence-backed coherent package → Cardcade readiness → isolated test.

Cardcade remains evidence/testing owner and must not redesign the candidate.
